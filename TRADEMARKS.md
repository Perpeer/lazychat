# The name lazychat and the mascot Lazy

The licence in `LICENSE` covers lazychat's code. It does not cover these,
which belong to perpeer and stay reserved:

- the name **lazychat**, and names close enough to be taken for it;
- the mascot **Lazy**: its drawings in `assets/` (the SVG, the icons, the
  screenshots and the demo), the menu bar app's icon and the mascot's
  frames drawn by the code (`internal/ui/kit/mascot*.go`,
  `macos/Lazychat/mascot.swift`);
- Lazy's sounds (`internal/core/sound/*.wav`).

You may:

- say that your work uses, is based on, or works with lazychat, and link
  to https://github.com/perpeer/lazychat;
- show lazychat's name and Lazy when you write or talk about lazychat
  itself.

You may not:

- give a changed copy, a fork, or another product the name lazychat, or
  ship it with Lazy, its drawings or its sounds: a fork takes its own
  name and leaves them out;
- use the name or Lazy so that your work looks made or endorsed by
  lazychat's author.

To ask for anything else, open an issue at
https://github.com/perpeer/lazychat/issues.
